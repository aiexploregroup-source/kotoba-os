## 7. ANIMATION NOTES

- Separate layers: back hair / body / front hair / head / eyes / brows / mouth / arms / legs
- Hair animation: forelock and cowlick overlap slightly into the next frame (follow-through), with a 2–3 frame lag on quick head turns
- Head turns: redraw the forelock for each angle rather than stretching it
- Line weight consistent; avoid sketchy strokes in final frames
- Palette stays identical under all lighting — shade with darker teal/coral, not hue shifts
- Export PNG with transparent background for compositing
