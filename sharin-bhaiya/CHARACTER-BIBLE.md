# SHARIN BHAIYA — Official Character Bible (v2)

The tall, glasses-wearing, dark-smart redesign. This is now the canon design.
Every future image, thumbnail, short and episode must match this document.

---

## 1. IDENTITY

- **Name:** Sharin Bhaiya
- **Height:** 6'5" (196 cm)
- **Age / role:** 25-year-old elder-brother figure — the smart, calm one who explains things
- **Vibe:** dark-academia handsome, quietly confident, witty, warm underneath the cool
- **Art direction:** modern anime character design with clean Western 2D animation shapes
- **Rendering:** crisp controlled line art, cel shading, soft rim light, consistent upper-left key light
- **Never:** 3D render, photoreal, chibi, muscle-bound, messy clutter

---

## 2. SILHOUETTE & PROPORTIONS

- **7.5–8 heads tall** — long legs, narrow waist, relaxed shoulders
- Shoulders ≈ 2.2 head widths; slim-athletic, not bulky
- Long straight coat breaks the silhouette at mid-thigh
- Head is small relative to the body — this is what sells "tall"

---

## 3. FACE (locked)

- Sharp, defined jaw with a clean chin; high cheekbones
- Narrow, slightly upturned anime eyes; dark espresso irises with a warm amber ring
- Thick straight brows, one usually a few degrees higher (his default expression)
- Slim straight nose, small confident mouth with one corner lifted
- **Signature: thin dark rectangular-round glasses with gold hinge dots** — always worn
- Warm tan skin `#C88A5A`, soft shadow `#A96E43`, highlight `#DFA877`

## 4. HAIR (locked)

- Espresso black `#15131C`, cool indigo highlight `#2E2A45`
- Layered anime cut: long side-swept fringe, sharp separated locks, tapered sides
- **Signature: one long strand falls across the right lens** (viewer-left is his right)
- Small sideburn points; hair sits above the collar
- Must be redrawn — not stretched — for each head angle

## 7. BRAND IDENTITY

- **Silhouette hook:** tall coat line + small head + long fringe strand
- **Signature expression:** one brow raised, half-smile, eyes calm behind glasses
- **Signature pose:** three-quarter stance, one hand loosely raised while explaining
- **Emblem:** circular comet — gold spark with a short coral tail

## 8. EXPRESSION SYSTEM (15)

## 10. POSE LIBRARY

## 11. ANIMATION LAYERS

`#back-hair` · `#leg-left` · `#leg-right` · `#torso` · `#arm-left` + `#forearm-left` · `#arm-right` + `#forearm-right` · `#ears` · `#head` · `#glasses` · `#front-hair`

- Elbow joints are separate groups so arms can bend
- Hair follows through 2–3 frames late on fast head turns
- Keep line weight constant; shade with darker values, never hue shifts
- Export transparent PNG for compositing
